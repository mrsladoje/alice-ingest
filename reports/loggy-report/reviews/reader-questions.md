# Reader test questions

A fresh reader gets only the report text and these questions. The answers are then checked against the briefs.

1. An O2 process on an EPN writes an info-level line. Where does that line end up, how many copies exist, and who can still read it if that EPN dies?
2. The collector on one worker dies. What happens between that moment and a person being told, and how long does it take?
3. Why did the project reject a message queue between the collector and the store, and what would reopen that decision?
4. The report mentions Kafka twice. What is the difference between the two decisions, and which one is agreed?
5. How much of a worker does the platform take (processor, memory), and where do those numbers come from?
6. Changing the flush interval from 5 seconds to 1 second changed what, by how much, in what unit, measured on which machine?
7. What is a template, and what does the stamper do to a record before it is indexed?
8. Which parts of the described system are built and running today, and which are agreed and not built?
9. What does the number 0.685 mean in the semantic retrieval result, and what is it compared against?
10. Why can the report's cost figures not be transferred directly to the farm, and what single measurement would fix that?
11. What are the six objectives the platform had to meet, and which design element meets each?
12. What is the difference between a detector, a monitor, an episode and a notification?
