What we
want to build

Six objectives shape the design that follows. Each one names a property the platform must have. Together they keep the volume where it is made, and the value where it is safe.

No bulk over the wire

Application logs at info severity are most of the volume. Each worker indexes its own on its own disk. They don’t cross the network.

Split by severity

We split some log families by severity, not by source. That puts the volume on one side and the value on the other. Each kind of log then gets its own keep time and its own number of copies.

Distributed

Five machines share the work as one cluster. Together they handle more throughput than any one machine could.

Durable

Some logs matter more than others. We need a storage tier that can ensure higher replication and retention policies for them.

Workers stay cheap

A worker indexes its own logs and answers a query now and then. It never manages the cluster. It takes four of the 128 cores an EPN has, and little memory. We must not disturb the expensive timeframe reconstruction.

Fault tolerant

Two of the three storage machines are enough to keep going. One can die and we lose nothing. In production the tier is bigger, so more can die. Lose a worker and we lose only its local logs.

Every worker pushes its own numbers up, and nothing goes out to ask. Three lanes read those numbers. Many hits about one machine fold into one alert.
